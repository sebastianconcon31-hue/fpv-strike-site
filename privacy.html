Privacy Policy

FPV Strike, by FableStudios. Last updated 7 October 2026.

In short: FPV Strike does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the game keeps

Your settings (flight mode, camera look, view size, volume, theme, last map and time of day) are saved in a small file on your headset. That file never leaves your headset. Uninstalling the game removes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or read your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • It does not record your play or your voice, and it does not send anything about you to us or to anyone else.
  • It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • It has no social features and no online multiplayer.

Meta

FPV Strike is sold through the Meta Horizon Store. When you launch it, the game asks Meta's platform to confirm that your Meta account owns the app. That check is done by Meta, using the Meta Platform SDK, and Meta handles the information involved under Meta's own privacy policy. We do not receive your account details. Meta also provides your headset's standard tracking and controller input, which the game uses only to play, on the headset.

Children

The game is rated for teens and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds online features, this page will say so before that version is released.
