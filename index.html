FPV STRIKE

A VR strike-drone game for Meta Quest, by FableStudios.

Fly a 7-inch FPV strike drone across five war-zone maps, by day, dusk or night. On the mission, destroy the artillery and the ammunition depot before your eight drones run out. In free flight, take any map with nothing shooting back.

It flies like a real quad: Acro mode, no auto-hover, the radio in your hands, goggles or immersive view. Single player, Touch controllers. Comfort rating: Intense.

Maps

  • Frozen Front: snowbound trenches and a shelled village
  • Black Earth: autumn steppe, a river and a village in the rain
  • Iron Town: ruined flats, a steelworks and a canal
  • Ridgeline: pine slopes, a quarry and a dam above the valley
  • Practice Field: six timed drills, a lit race course and a freestyle park
